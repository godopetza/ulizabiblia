UlizaBiblia

Sera ya faragha · Privacy policy

Imesasishwa / Updated: 26 Novemba 2026

UlizaBiblia ni app na tovuti ya kujifunza Biblia, inayotolewa na Godopetza Labs Company Limited, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania (“sisi”). Sera hii inaeleza taarifa tunazokusanya, kwa nini, na haki zako. English follows each section.

1. Taarifa tunazokusanya · What we collect

  • Akaunti: barua pepe au namba ya simu unayotumia kuingia; ukiingia kwa Google au Apple, tunapokea kitambulisho cha akaunti hiyo na barua pepe (Apple inaweza kukupa barua pepe ya siri). Account: the email or phone number you sign in with; with Google or Apple sign-in we receive that account’s ID and email (Apple may give a private relay address).
  • Wasifu (hiari isipokuwa jina): jina, jinsia na tarehe ya kuzaliwa, lugha, tafsiri ya Biblia, mambo unayopenda na saa ya mstari wa siku. Profile: name (required), and optionally gender and date of birth; language, Bible translation, interests and reminder time.
  • Maswali na majibu: maswali unayomuuliza Daudi na majibu yake, ili uone historia yako. Your questions to Daudi and its answers, so you can see your history.
  • Shughuli zako: mipango ya kusoma, quiz, alama za mistari, daftari la maombi na mfululizo wa siku. Reading plans, quizzes, highlights, prayer journal and streaks.
  • Malipo: ukilipia Plus kwa pesa ya simu, namba ya simu ya malipo, mtandao, kiasi na hali ya malipo. Hatuoni wala kuhifadhi PIN yako. Payments: for Plus via mobile money, the paying phone number, network, amount and status. We never see or store your PIN.

Hatukusanyi mahali ulipo, anwani za simu, picha wala matangazo. Hakuna matangazo ndani ya app. We do not collect location, contacts or photos, and there are no ads.

2. Tunavyozitumia · How we use it

  • Kukuingiza na kuhifadhi akaunti yako. To sign you in and keep your account.
  • Kujibu maswali yako: swali lako (pamoja na jina lako la kwanza na jinsia ikiwa umeziweka) hutumwa kwa mtoa huduma wetu wa AI, Anthropic (Claude), ili kutoa jibu. Anthropic haitumii taarifa hizi kufundisha mifumo yake. To answer your questions: your question (with your first name and gender if you set them) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to produce the answer. Anthropic does not train its models on this data.
  • Kuchagua mistari na mipango inayokufaa (umri na mambo unayopenda). To pick verses and plans that suit you.
  • Kushughulikia malipo ya Plus. To process Plus payments.

Hatuuzi taarifa zako kwa mtu yeyote. We never sell your data.

3. Tunaowashirikisha · Who we share with

  • Anthropic — majibu ya AI · AI answers.
  • Malipo (lango letu la malipo) na mitandao ya pesa ya simu (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, Mixx by Yas) — kwa malipo tu · payments only.
  • Resend — kutuma namba ya siri kwa barua pepe; WhatsApp (Meta) — kutuma namba ya siri kwa namba ya simu · sign-in codes.
  • Google, Apple — ukichagua kuingia nao · if you choose their sign-in.
  • Railway (seva na hifadhidata) · hosting and database.

4. Arifa · Notifications

Ukiruhusu, simu yako itakukumbusha mstari wa siku kwa saa uliyochagua. Ukumbusho huu hupangwa ndani ya simu yako. Unaweza kuuzima wakati wowote kwenye Mimi → Mstari wa siku. If you allow it, your phone reminds you about the daily verse at your chosen time. It is scheduled on your device and can be turned off in Me → Verse of the day.

5. Muda wa kuhifadhi na kufuta · Retention and deletion

Tunahifadhi taarifa zako mpaka ufute akaunti. Unaweza kufuta akaunti yako ndani ya app (Mimi → Futa akaunti) au kwa kutuandikia. Kufuta huondoa maswali, mipango, maombi na wasifu wako mara moja. Rekodi za malipo huhifadhiwa kwa sababu za kihasibu bila namba yako ya simu. We keep your data until you delete your account, in the app (Me → Delete account) or by emailing us. Deletion removes your questions, plans, prayers and profile immediately; payment records are kept for accounting without your phone number.

6. Watoto · Children

UlizaBiblia haijalengwa kwa watoto chini ya miaka 13. Hatukusanyi taarifa za watoto kwa makusudi. UlizaBiblia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Usalama · Security

Mawasiliano yote yanatumia HTTPS. Namba za siri za kuingia huhifadhiwa kama hash na huisha baada ya dakika 10. All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in codes are stored hashed and expire after 10 minutes.

8. Haki zako · Your rights

Unaweza kuomba nakala ya taarifa zako, kuzirekebisha au kuzifuta. You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it or delete it.
